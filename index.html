Who We Are


ALG Transport And Logistics, formerly Addison Lee Ghana Limited, is a Ghanaian owned transport and logistics company managed by owners with local and international expertise, knowledge and experience with a combined industry know-how of twenty five years.


ALG's rich directorial experience stems from the car rental industry in Ghana and private car hire and dispatch services from Addison Lee Limited in London. We use technology to improve the experience of all involved, from office staff and drivers to customers.

Frequently asked questions about ALG Logistics services

How far in advance should I book a service?

For most bookings, 24 to 48 hours in advance is ideal. For airport pickups, bus rentals, and long-term lease requests, we recommend 3 to 7 days notice so we can secure the best vehicle and schedule. Same-day bookings are handled based on availability.

What services does ALG Logistics provide?

We provide self-drive car rentals, airport pickups, hourly chauffeur service, pick and drop transport, long-term lease plans, courier services, and bus rentals for group movement.

Do you track flights for airport pickups?

Yes. Our airport team monitors flight status and adjusts pickup timing for delays or early arrivals where possible. Driver details are shared before pickup for easier coordination.

Can businesses get monthly or long-term lease plans?

Yes. We offer flexible lease durations for individuals and companies, including monthly and longer contract terms. Plans can be structured around fleet size, usage needs, and service support requirements.

Are your vehicles and deliveries insured?

Coverage terms differ by service type. Our vehicles operate under the required commercial protections, and courier deliveries follow documented handling and proof-of-delivery procedures. Our team will share exact coverage options during booking.

How does your courier service work?

After your request is confirmed, we schedule pickup, verify sender and receiver details, and dispatch your package through our courier network. You receive status updates, and completion is confirmed with proof of delivery.

Do you provide bus rentals for events and staff transport?

Yes. We provide bus rental solutions for staff transport, school and group movement, tours, and special events. Both one-time trips and recurring schedules can be arranged.

Which areas do you serve and what are your operating hours?

We support city and intercity routes based on booking demand. Standard operations run daily, and early morning or late-night trips can be arranged in advance, especially for airport transfers and scheduled corporate transport.
